How to Extract Specific Pages From a PDF

Extracting pages from large reports, books, or scanned multi-page invoices often presents a dilemma: you want just a few pages, but third-party tools require you to hand over the entire document to their servers.

With InstantFileTools, your PDF is loaded directly into your browser's V8 JavaScript runtime. The engine parses the PDF object cross-reference table, extracts only the selected page indices, and packs them into a lightweight new document without any server-side upload.

Supported Extraction Formats

  • Continuous Range: Type 1-5 to grab the first 5 pages of the file.
  • Discrete Pages: Type 1, 3, 7 to extract specific non-adjacent pages.
  • Hybrid Selections: Type 1-3, 5, 8-10 to combine both individual pages and clusters.
