Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 10, 2026

1. Client-Side Processing Architecture (Zero File Storage)

At InstantFileTools (accessible from https://instantfiletools.online), user privacy is our foundational principle. Unlike standard cloud conversion platforms, our tools operate 100% within your local web browser using client-side JavaScript, WebAssembly, and native Canvas APIs.

Your documents, images, and PDF files are never uploaded to our servers, stored in any database, or inspected by automated scanners. Once you finish processing and close or refresh your browser tab, all temporary file buffers are instantly purged from your device’s volatile memory (RAM).

2. Cookies and Third-Party Advertising

We partner with third-party advertising vendors, including Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website. Google uses cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites across the internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings or by navigating to aboutads.info.

3. Analytics and Log Files

Like many web applications, we use standard analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to monitor aggregate traffic patterns, browser types, operating systems, and page load performance. This telemetry contains no personally identifiable document data and is used solely to enhance system responsiveness and layout compatibility.

4. Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our client-side processing architecture, contact our technical team via our Contact Page.
