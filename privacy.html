Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Client-Side File Processing & Zero Storage

InstantFileTools processes all documents, PDFs, and images locally inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas and WebAssembly technologies. We do not upload, transfer, view, or store your files on any remote server. Your files remain strictly on your device and are cleared from memory when you close or reload the browser.

2. Cookies & Advertising

We use third-party advertising services (such as Google AdSense) to serve advertisements when you visit our website. These vendors may use cookies, web beacons, or unique device identifiers to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

3. Analytics & Usage Logs

We may collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic information such as browser type, operating system, and referring URLs to monitor website performance and prevent abuse.

4. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact Page.
