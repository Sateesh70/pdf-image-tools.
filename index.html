Why Use In-Browser Document Processing?

Traditional online PDF mergers and image compressors require you to upload your sensitive documents and images to remote web servers. This raises data privacy concerns, consumes internet bandwidth, and limits file upload sizes.

DocuFix uses modern WebAssembly and JavaScript standards to process files directly inside your browser memory. This means your private bank statements, contracts, IDs, and photos are never uploaded or stored anywhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there any file size limit?

Because processing depends on your local device RAM and browser capabilities, you can process high-resolution images and large PDFs without cloud storage restrictions.

Which image formats provide the best compression?

WebP offers up to 30% higher compression efficiency compared to standard JPEG while preserving image sharpness and transparency.
