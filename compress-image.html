Compress Images Under 50 KB, 100 KB, or 200 KB

Many official portals, competitive exam application systems, and government forms require photo uploads to meet strict file size ceilings (often under 20 KB or 50 KB). Standard image editors can make it frustrating to hit these targets without completely ruining readability.

Our compressor operates by feeding your image into an offscreen HTML5 Canvas context. By adjusting the lossy DCT compression quantization matrices in the toBlob() pipeline, you can fine-tune file weight directly without having to upload photos to a remote computer.

Recommended Quality Guidelines

  • Under 50 KB (Official Forms): Set slider to 30%–45%.
  • Web Optimization (Blogs & E-commerce): Set slider to 65%–75% for visual clarity with up to 80% reduction in file size.
  • Portfolio & Photography: Set slider to 80%–85% to strip excess metadata while keeping high-resolution fidelity.
