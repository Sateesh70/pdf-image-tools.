How to Electronically Sign PDF Documents Online

  1. Load Agreement: Drag your document into the upload dropzone or select it from your device.
  2. Draw Signature: Use your mouse, touchpad, or touchscreen stylus on the drawing pad. Choose between legal black or formal blue ink.
  3. Choose Destination Page & Coordinates: Designate whether the signature should appear on the first page, the concluding signature page, or all pages, and pick your preferred placement zone.
  4. Embed & Download: Click Stamp Signature & Download PDF. The signature bitmap is encoded into a transparent PNG vector stream and saved inside the PDF file.

Why Security Matters When Signing PDFs

Agreements, employment offer letters, NDAs, leases, and procurement invoices represent your most sensitive documents. Handing them over to third-party web signature platforms creates unnecessary exposure to security breaches, data harvesting, and server leaks.

InstantFileTools runs completely inside your browser's execution sandbox. The signature drawn on the HTML5 canvas is converted directly to an in-memory transparent raster array and embedded into the PDF using pdf-lib. Not a single byte of your signature or agreement ever touches an external network server.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this signature legally acceptable?

Yes, standard electronic signatures (eSignatures) created by drawing a mark are widely valid for commercial agreements, receipts, and internal authorizations in jurisdictions adhering to ESIGN and UETA standards.

Does the signature have a transparent background?

Yes. The drawing canvas exports only the ink stroke pixels, keeping the background 100% alpha transparent so it does not obscure underlying document lines.
