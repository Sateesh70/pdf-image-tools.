How to Watermark PDF Documents Online

  1. Select Document: Drag and drop your PDF into the upload zone or choose a file from your device.
  2. Customize Stamp Attributes: Enter your custom watermark text (e.g., "COPY", "DO NOT DISTRIBUTE"), choose diagonal or horizontal orientation, and fine-tune opacity so it protects the file without obscuring document readability.
  3. Download Modified PDF: Click Apply Watermark & Download PDF. The vector text is merged into each page stream and saved to your device immediately.

Why Use In-Browser Watermarking?

Watermarking is typically used on sensitive material—such as unreleased financial audits, proprietary schematics, employment contracts, or identity documentation. Uploading these documents to public web servers to stamp a watermark undermines the very confidentiality you are attempting to protect.

InstantFileTools runs vector operations through pdf-lib directly within your browser. The engine calculates the geometric center of each page bounding box (mediabox) and stamps the vector text characters with precise alpha blending. The source file stays strictly in your computer's RAM.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the watermark be easily erased?

Our tool injects the text directly into the page's structural content stream, making it a permanent part of the document rather than a superficial web overlay.

What is the optimal opacity for background stamps?

We recommend setting opacity between 20% and 30%. This ensures the watermark is distinct while keeping underlying text and data fully readable.
